## Decision

The frozen gate remains **FAIL**. The pilot supports drafting a new v1-only controlled-data protocol with positive scenario coverage checked before sampling and the tested whitespace fix. It does not justify training performance claims, natural-workflow claims or silently excluding v2 from the old denominator. Exact runner/site provenance remains unknown. No follow-up collection or model training has started.
