## Execution and artifact evidence

The [session schema](https://github.com/risenlab/agentlogs/blob/0af19d213339e26d0f3e447be11cd2d73d5ea505/docs/schema/agent_session.md) describes `base.ref` as the branch started from and `head.ref` as the branch created or pushed to. These are refs, not immutable SHAs. A PR's collected base/head SHA is a snapshot and does not establish a session's original checkout or its sole output.

For the earlier Connectbot candidate, [log rows 0-99](https://datasets-server.huggingface.co/rows?dataset=risenlab%2Fagentlogs&config=agent_session_logs&split=train&offset=0&length=100) and [100-199](https://datasets-server.huggingface.co/rows?dataset=risenlab%2Fagentlogs&config=agent_session_logs&split=train&offset=100&length=100) contain contiguous indices 0-54 for session `d593fe42-565b-4f9d-9160-0e20f3696a24` and 0-70 for `b4a04888-dbf6-406c-8ecc-1738d8100b82`, followed by other sessions. Their log entries record commits and pushes, respectively `87118bd` and `ac1654c`, and later report the branches up to date. GitHub resolves them to [87118bdc6fcf9f19086475d50af08b2f23fd6d7b](https://api.github.com/repos/connectbot/connectbot/commits/87118bd) and [ac1654c4459a8159353f3d0ecae1c88ec1dde4bc](https://api.github.com/repos/connectbot/connectbot/commits/ac1654c). Their immediate parent commits have tree IDs `31ef56570707ede84b5df591441b6cea68f94478` and `993add2c11e528cda592b86b728f6c8420cd0966`, respectively. These are different commit-parent trees, not verified initial execution trees. The inspected entries do not record a checkout or `git rev-parse` identifying either actual starting tree. The logs support attribution of these two commits to the respective sessions, but do not prove they constitute complete independent final outputs from a common starting tree. Contiguous viewer entries and lack of an obvious partner message do not prove the upstream logs or initial context are complete. The sessions started nine days apart, which does not establish that both tasks were available and unstarted at a shared decision point.

Result: **0 valid common-base distinct-task pairs established** among the two selected sample candidates or the prior Connectbot candidate; **0 pairs** with both complete session-specific outputs and no-partner evidence established. The jdbi prompt is sampled negative evidence for the desired output type. Cassandra and Connectbot provenance remain unresolved; inability to locate Cassandra logs is an access limitation, not proof that suitable records do not exist. No outcome labels were inspected or produced.
